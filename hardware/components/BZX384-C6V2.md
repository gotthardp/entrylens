## Electrical limits and consumption

| Parameter | Value | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Working voltage (VZ) | 5.80 to 6.60 V | IZ = 5 mA, Tj = 25 degC |
| Differential resistance (rdif) | 150 Ohm max / 10 Ohm max | IZ = 1 mA / IZ = 5 mA |
| Reverse current (IR) | 3 uA max | VR = 4 V |
| Temperature coefficient (SZ) | 0.4 to 3.7 mV/K | IZ = 5 mA |
| Forward voltage (VF) | 0.9 V max / 1.1 V max | IF = 10 mA / IF = 100 mA |
| Forward current (IF) | 250 mA | Limiting value |
| Non-repetitive peak reverse current (IZSM) | 6.0 A | tp = 100 us square wave, Tj = 25 degC prior to surge |
| Total power dissipation (Ptot) | 300 mW | Tamb <= 25 degC, FR4 PCB, standard footprint |
| Junction temperature (Tj) | 150 degC | Limiting value |
| Thermal resistance | 415 K/W junction to ambient, 110 K/W junction to solder point | In free air on FR4 / cathode tab |
