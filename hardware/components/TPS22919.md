# Pin requirements: TPS22919

- References:
  - Datasheet: `tps22919.pdf`, SLVSEN5B, October 2018, revised May 2019. Pin table in section 5;
    ratings in 6.1 to 6.4; electrical and switching characteristics in 6.5 and 6.6; ON-pin and
    QOD behaviour in 8.3.1 and 8.3.3; supply guidance in section 10.
- Package / orderable variant this table applies to: SC-70-6 (DCK package), TPS22919DCKR

## Per-pin requirements

| Pin | Function | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (IN) | Switch input | 1.6 to 5.5 V operating, 6 V absolute maximum. An input capacitance of 1 uF is sufficient in most cases and must be placed as close to the pin as possible; additional bulk is needed only where the supply responds slowly to a large turn-on transient. |
| 2 (GND) | Device ground | |
| 3 (ON) | Active-high switch control input | **"Do not leave floating."** VIH 1 V minimum, VIL 0.35 V maximum, so a 3.3 V GPIO drives it directly. A Smart Pull Down holds the pin low when VIN is first applied, but it disconnects permanently once the pin is driven high - see the section below. |
| 4 (NC) | No connect | Datasheet: "No connect pin, leave floating." |
| 5 (QOD) | Quick output discharge | Three sanctioned configurations: tie directly to VOUT and use the internal RPD(QOD); connect to VOUT through an external RQOD to slow the discharge; or leave floating, which disables discharge entirely and leaves the output floating after turn-off. |
| 6 (VOUT) | Switch output | 0 to 5.5 V operating, 6 V absolute maximum. |

Self-protected: current limit, short-circuit protection and thermal shutdown. No figure from those
is used by this design.

Switching characteristics are typical values at VIN = 3.6 V, TA = 25 degC, CL = 0.1 uF and
RL = 100 Ohm unless the row says otherwise. Turn-on slew scales with input voltage: 1.8 mV/us at
1.8 V, 2.7 mV/us at 3.6 V, 3.2 mV/us at 5.0 V.

## Smart ON pull down - one-shot, not a permanent pull-down

Section 8.3.1 and Table 1. When power is first applied to VIN, a 530 kOhm Smart Pull Down holds
the ON pin low so the switch stays off while system sequencing completes. **Once ON is
deliberately driven to VIH or above, the pull down is disconnected** to avoid wasting current,
and it does not re-arm until VIN is cycled.

The consequence is that the pull down protects the cold-start case only. If the controlling GPIO
can ever return to high impedance while VIN stays up - an MCU reset that does not cycle the rail,
a low-power mode, or a pin reconfiguration - the ON pin is left floating on 100 nA of leakage,
which is exactly what the pin table forbids, and the switch state becomes undefined.

## Output discharge and fall time

With QOD tied straight to VOUT the discharge resistance is the internal 24 Ohm. With an external
resistor between VOUT and QOD the two add:

    RDIS = RPD(QOD) + RQOD

and the resulting fall time, 90 % to 10 %, is:

    tFALL = 2.2 x (RDIS || RL) x CL

with RDIS and RL in Ohm, CL in uF and tFALL in us. Leaving QOD floating disables discharge
altogether and the output floats after turn-off.
