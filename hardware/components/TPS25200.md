# Pin requirements: TPS25200

- References:
  - Datasheet: `tps25200.pdf`, Table 4-1 (Pin Functions)
  - Exposed-pad land, stencil aperture and thermal via design (app note SLUA271C): `slua271c.pdf`
- Package / orderable variant this table applies to: WSON-6 (DRV package), TPS25200DRVR

## Per-pin requirements

| Pin | Function | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (OUT) | Protected power switch output, O | No specific termination stated beyond normal output routing. |
| 2 (ILIM) | Current-limit set, O | External resistor to GND sets the current-limit threshold. Recommended 33 kOhm <= RILIM <= 1100 kOhm. |
| 3 (FAULT) | Fault indicator, O | Active-low open-drain output, asserted during overcurrent, overvoltage, or overtemperature. Connect a pullup resistor to the logic I/O voltage. |
| 4 (EN) | Enable, I | Logic-level control input; driven high enables the power switch, low disables it. Cannot be left floating, and must be limited below the absolute maximum rating if tied to VIN. Section 7.3.1: "EN can be tied to VIN with a pull up resistor, and is protected with an integrated zener diode. Use a sufficiently large (300-kOhm) pull up resistor". See the internal EN zener note below. |
| 5 (GND) | Ground | Connect externally to PowerPAD. |
| 6 (IN) | Input voltage, I | Connect a 0.1 uF or greater ceramic capacitor from IN to GND as close to the IC as possible. |
| PAD (PowerPAD) | Thermal/ground pad | Internally connected to GND; used to heat-sink the part to the board. Connect PowerPAD to the GND terminal externally. |

Datasheet note: electrical characteristics conditions are -40 to 125 degC and 2.5-6.5 V VIN,
VEN = VIN, typical values at 25 degC. The Electrical Characteristics table lists six RILIM
values; the two retained above bracket this design's setting. Section 8.2.2.3, Equation 1
covers the whole recommended range 33 kOhm <= RILIM <= 1100 kOhm. With RILIM in kOhm and the
result in mA:

    IOS(max) = 96754 / RILIM^0.985 + 30
    IOS(nom) = 98322 / RILIM^1.003
    IOS(min) = 97399 / RILIM^1.015 - 30

The exponents sit on RILIM. A PDF text extraction flattens the superscripts into multipliers
and then yields wrong answers, so read them from the rendered page. The datasheet calls these
an approximation of the threshold for a given resistor; they reproduce the six tabulated rows
to within a few percent. The resistor's own tolerance is separate and must be applied on top:
evaluate IOS(min) at RILIM +1 % and IOS(max) at RILIM -1 %.

Internal EN zener: note A of the Functional Block Diagram reads in full "6.4-V Typical Clamp
Voltage" - **no minimum, no maximum**, and no row in the Electrical Characteristics. With EN
rated 7 V and IN 20 V, pulling EN up to IN relies on that typical alone. For a sustained input
above the EN rating, add an external clamp that conducts below 6.4 V.
